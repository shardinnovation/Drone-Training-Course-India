# Drone Training Course

## Shard Center for Innovation

Learn the fundamentals of drone technology, drone flying, safety, flight planning, and real-world applications through practical and project-based learning.

Course reference:
https://scilindia.org/courses/Drone-training-course

---

## About the Drone Training Course

The Drone Training Course at Shard Center for Innovation (SCIL) is designed to introduce students and beginners to drone technology through practical learning.

The program combines theoretical understanding with simulator-based practice and guided live drone flying sessions.

Students learn how drones work, how they are controlled, how to fly them safely, and how drone technology is used in real-world applications.

---

## Who Can Learn Drone Technology?

The program is designed for beginners and school students, including learners in Grades 6–12, who are interested in drones, technology, engineering, STEM education, and innovation.

No previous drone flying experience is required for beginner-level learning.

---

## What You Will Learn

The course introduces learners to:

- Introduction to drones and their components
- Basic drone flight concepts
- Drone controls
- Flight planning
- Simulator-based training
- Manual and GPS-based flying
- Drone safety practices
- DGCA rules and safety guidelines
- Aerial photography and videography concepts
- Real-world drone applications
- Troubleshooting and flight-related problem solving
- Industry applications and future career opportunities

## Practical Drone Flight Training

The learning approach includes practical activities rather than only theoretical lessons.

Students can experience:

The objective is to help learners develop confidence and responsible drone handling skills.
