Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is a Drone Training Course?

It is a practical course in which you will learn about drone operation, how they fly in safe conditions and how they are applied in such fields as photography, delivery, and agriculture.

2. Who should take this course?

Students who want to know more about drone technology, hobbyists or professionals with an interest in aerial photography and careers in drones in the future.

3. Is certification mandatory to fly drones in India?

Yes in case of commercial flying. Our curriculum prepares you towards certification as well as for the development of flying skills safely.

4. What will I learn in this Drone Training course?

You are going to learn how to fly a drone, flight techniques, safety strategy, and troubleshooting frequent problems.

5. Is this course DGCA-approved?

Our course is meant to prepare you against DGCA rules and exams though it is suitable for a beginner. To have a professional certification, you will have to enroll with an authorised training school of DGCA.

6. Are both theory and practical classes included?

Yes. Among other things, you will study the theory that underlies the operation of a drone and fly the drones under the guidance of an expert.

7. What are the eligibility criteria for drone training?

Eligible candidates should be of age, above 14 years old. You do not have to have prior flying experience.

8. What kind of drones will I train with?

You will practice with the drones that are easier to fly, on the level of beginners, such as quadcopters, which allow training to fly safely.
